About

The Sound
of Istanbul

Born in Istanbul in 1991, Headonit (Baturay Kartal) is a DJ and producer blending the deep rhythms of Afro House with the hypnotic energy of Afro Tech.

With 16 years of classical piano background and over three years of electronic music production, Headonit crafts immersive soundscapes that balance raw emotion with powerful grooves. His music reflects a journey between tradition and modernity — where ancestral rhythms meet futuristic textures.
